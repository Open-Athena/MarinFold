Choosing one complete rollout does not improve mean accuracy. Pooled votes score 55.8% F1; even the truth-selected best of 200 samples averages 55.0%. The paired difference is −0.75 points (95% interval −2.00 to +0.57). Pooled voting beats the best individual sample on 62 of 97 proteins. The average individual map scores 41.5%; consensus and likelihood selection reach 49.0% and 46.7%.

Many errors are already present within the samples. The best samples for 7wz5_A, 8arl_A, 8bux_A and 8dmu_A reach only 9.3%, 13.1%, 11.2% and 10.5% F1. Long-range patterns remain missing or incomplete. Some proteins do have better individual maps: 7t9r_A improves from 29.2% pooled to 52.6% oracle, and 7xp9_A from 28.9% to 50.0%. The gallery starts with the largest oracle gains so these exceptions are visible.

The clustering result needs care. Oracle cluster voting at k=4 scores 55.7%, effectively tied with pooled voting. But average-linkage Jaccard clustering mostly leaves one large group: only 37 of 194 swapped-pool fits have two or more eligible test clusters. This is limited evidence about separating useful modes, not a general test of every geometric or learned grouping. The result primarily points to limited whole-map sample quality under this recipe and budget; it does not identify a training cause.
